Curriculum Vitae

Hi, I'm Nilou. I'm a first-year PhD student and Presidential Scholar in the Department of Government at Harvard University. Before that, I received my BA (2025) in Political Science and History from Stanford University.

My research studies the political economy of identity and migration, with a geographic focus on Western Europe. I also work on computational methods for video data.

You can contact me by email at niloudavis@g.harvard.edu.
